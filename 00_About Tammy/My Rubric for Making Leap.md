I have a bit of a framework I apply to these types of situations to help me stay sane. It usually lives in my head and isn't something that I'm super dogmatic about but I find that I often anchor on it.

  

It goes like this:

1. Am I bought into the vision and is it something I can apply my ambitions to.
    
2. Do I believe I have the abilities and the empowerment to contribute to the vision? Empowerment is a key factor for me.
    
3. Do I see opportunities for me to personally learn and grow?
    
4. What are my non-negotiables and how will I know if there is clear signal that I need to exit?
    

  

My non-negotiable is that my empowerment gets stripped. If that happens, I can't stick around. If I stay empowered, I can keep moving.
