**7 Habits** 

1. Be proactive
    
2. Begin with the end in mind
    
3. Put first things first
    
4. Think win-win
    
5. Seek first to understand then to be understood
    
6. Synergize
    
7. Sharpen the saw
    

**8 Commandments**

1. Do not hurt yourself or others
    
2. Do not lie to yourself or others
    
3. Do not take stuff that is not yours or that belongs to everyone
    
4. Talk respectfully, gently, and kindly to every person especially those smaller and older than you
    
5. Smile
    
6. Pick up trash that crosses your path
    
7. Care for animals that are injured and people who are hurt
    
8. Don’t hold onto hurt or anger or people you don’t love or who don’t love you
    

**9 Epiphanies**

1. You are not your mind
    
2. Life unfolds in moments
    
3. Quality of life is determined by how you deal with your moments, not which moments
    
4. Most of life is imaginary
    
5. Human beings have evolved to suffer and we are better at that than anything else
    
6. Emotions exist to make us biased
    
7. All people operate from the same two motivations: to fulfill their desire and to escape their suffering
    
8. Beliefs are nothing to be proud of
    
9. Objectivity is subjective
    

**10 isms**

1. Remember to have fun!
    
2. You are nature’s greatest creation
    
3. Taking care of your body is more important than a lot of things
    
4. Focus on things you can control
    
5. Be the best thing in someone’s day
    
6. Move forward with grace
    
7. You occupy a rare discursive space
    
8. always offer your best
    
9. You have no idea what the person in front of you has been through or what they feel and think
    
10. Too much of a good thing is not a good thing

**4 Agreements**
1. Be impeccable in your word
2. Don’t make assumptions
3. Don’t take things personally
4. Always try your best
