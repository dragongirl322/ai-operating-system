Someone asked me recently what I think about craft. I have been left feeling rather dissatisfied with my response and slightly embarrassed that in my response, I failed to demonstrate craft. 

With that in mind, I set aside time to be intentional and thoughtful in defining what craft means to me. I've concluded that craft is foundational to every aspect of my work as a leader, a human centered design thinker and doer, and as a person.  Further, I've been reminded that craft is critical to everything we do as a UX practice at Workday.

**What Craft Means to Me**

1. **Honor people:** At the heart of every design decision is people. To craft is to understand their goals, behaviors, and needs deeply. Great design begins and ends with people in mind.
    
2. **Strive for Mastery:** Mastery is a journey, not a destination. It is the constant pursuit of growth, pushing boundaries, refining skills, and valuing the “near-perfect” as a catalyst for deeper understanding and innovation**.**  It requires that we embrace and use the tools of our trade with skill and efficiency while not being bound by them.
    
3. **Cherish Iteration:** The journey to excellence is non-linear. Craft demands resilience, curiosity, and a willingness to refine until we achieve clarity, balance, and utility.
    
4. **Balance Beauty and Functionality:** Craft thrives where form meets function. True excellence lies in creating experiences that are as delightful as they are effective, where beauty enhances purpose.
    
5. **Catalyze Collaboration:** Our unique strength lies in fostering collaboration that revolves around the human experience. We act as catalysts, bringing together diverse perspectives and disciplines, united by a shared focus on understanding and serving people. By anchoring every conversation and decision with the human at the center, we create synergy that leads to more meaningful and impactful outcomes.
    
6. **Create with Intent:** Every choice—big or small—must align with purpose. Craft is intentional, ensuring each detail contributes meaningfully to the whole.
