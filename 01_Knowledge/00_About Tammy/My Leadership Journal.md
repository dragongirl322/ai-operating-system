# 🚀 Tammy Snow — Personal Power Operating Code

### 1. I lead from a place of offering, not needing.

> I bring my unique gifts into every room. I am here to serve with strength, generosity, and strategic clarity.

### 2. I recognize dysfunction but do not submit to it.

> Dysfunction is the air, not the story. I stay grounded, strategic, and focused on driving value without getting pulled into frustration or blame.

### 3. I honor the rare and powerful space I occupy.

> My lived experience — age, identity, history, excellence — grants me a voice few others have. I lead with confident humility, never shrinking, never shouting, always steady.

### 4. I treat relationships and context as strategic assets.

> Influence travels through trust and shared understanding. I invest intentionally in human connection and environmental awareness.

### 5. I lead with adaptive mastery.

> I am a courageous learner. I refine, iterate, and invite others into solutions, not because I lack answers, but because partnership creates better answers.

### 6. I stand calm, candid, and bold — every day.

> I don’t rush, I don’t shrink, I don’t over explain. I cut through noise with clarity and kindness. I push boundaries with grace and strategic precision.

4 Roles of Leadership: (from Covey's 8th habit)

1. Modeling: set a good example (inspires trust without expecting it)
    
2. Pathfinding: jointly determine the course (creates order without demanding it)
    
3. Aligning: Set up and manage systems to stay on course (Nourishes both vision and empowerment without proclaiming them)
    
4. Empowering: Focus talent on results, not methods, then get out of people's way and give help as requested (unleashes human potential without externally motivating it)
