# TNT Music — Content & Posting Working Agreement

Between Tam and Groove (TNT Bomb / biz partner). Living doc — update when the process changes.

## Goal

Keep TNT Music visible and on-brand on Instagram, Facebook, and Google Business Profile without Tam carrying the daily create/post load alone.

## Sources of truth

1. **Brand strategy:** `TNT-Brand-Guide.md`
2. **Day-to-day copy:** `TNTMusic-Writing-Guide.md`
3. **Voice:** modern, confident, welcoming, savvy — music-savvy friend, not corporate
4. **Primary audience:** millennial vinyl lovers in Edmonds / North King / Snohomish

If copy and brand conflict, brand strategy wins.

## What Groove owns

- Plan content calendars (social + Google)
- Draft captions, creatives direction, and Google posts
- Post after Tam approves (or under a standing "auto-post this calendar" OK)
- Pull engagement metrics on a set cadence
- Suggest improvements, then implement only after Tam says go

## What Tam owns

- Final yes/no on anything that publishes or changes live settings
- Brand or business facts Groove shouldn't invent (events, prices, drops, hours)
- One-time (or rare) sign-in / 2FA on Groove's computer for IG, Facebook, and Google Business
- Photos/assets from the shop when a post needs something new

## Platforms

| Platform | Role | Cadence (default) |
|---|---|---|
| Instagram | Main social home | Daily or near-daily as agreed in the calendar |
| Facebook | Local / community mirror | Same or lighter; often adapted from IG |
| Google Business | Highlight reel for search/Maps | 1–2 posts/week (not daily) |

Google gets new arrivals, events, offers, hours/holiday notes, cleaning service — not pure vibes posts.

## Weekly rhythm

1. **Plan** — Groove proposes the next week's posts (platforms, hooks, assets needed).
2. **Approve** — Tam reviews in one pass (edit freely; silence is not approval).
3. **Make + schedule/post** — Groove drafts final copy, uses approved assets, posts or schedules.
4. **Note** — Quick log of what went live and when.

## Approval rules

- **Always ask before:** publishing, changing bio/about, deleting posts, running ads, changing GBP categories/attributes that affect how the business appears, or implementing metric-driven "improvements."
- **OK without re-asking each time:** drafting, researching, pulling public metrics, preparing a review of options.
- **Standing permission:** Tam can grant "post this week's approved calendar without another ping" — still revoke anytime.

## Metrics & improvements

- **Cadence:** biweekly review unless something spikes or tanks.
- **Look at:** reach, engagement rate, saves/shares, profile visits, Google views/actions when available, what themes win.
- **Output:** short "what's working / what's not / 2–3 recommended changes."
- **Action:** Groove only makes the changes after Tam approves them.

## Access

Posting and private insights run through signed-in sessions on Groove's computer (Instagram, Facebook Page, Google Business Profile). No Meta/Google Business connector yet. If a session expires, Tam re-auths once and we continue.

## Out of scope (unless Tam expands it)

- Paid ads spend without explicit approval
- Customer DMs / review replies (can draft; publishing needs OK unless standing permission is set)
- Inventory/Shopify ops (separate track)
