# Nutanix — design leader role

Researched 2026-08-26. Recruiter: Julie Baker (also recruited Tam to Workday). High-level briefing plus Tam’s read of the seat.

## The seat (from Tam)

Satish Ramachandran is retiring. This role is his replacement.

Satish is known as an engineer who became a design leader. There is desire to hire a *true* design leader who can help Nutanix become a more commercially viable company — not another eng-first design head.

Implication: the job is not “keep Prism pretty.” It is to make design a commercial lever (product story, buying experience, conversion, expansion) while the company rides a VMware-exit wave and tries to look like more than HCI. Ask, in interviews, how they will measure “commercially viable” and whether design has a seat with sales/product/GM — especially Indu Keri — or stays a craft function under eng.

## Business model

Software company, San Jose. Started as hyperconverged infrastructure (HCI). Now sells the Nutanix Cloud Platform as a hybrid/multicloud operating layer — on-prem, public cloud (NC2), Kubernetes (NKP), early AI infra. Almost all revenue is subscription (term licenses, support, SaaS). Hardware from partners (Lenovo, etc.). Pitch is choice: your servers, your storage, your cloud.

## Financials (FY26, IR / earnings)

- Q3 FY26 revenue ~$703M (+20% YoY)
- ARR ~$2.43B (+15%)
- Full-year revenue guide raised to ~$2.82–$2.84B
- Non-GAAP operating margin guide ~22.5%
- Free cash flow guide ~$760–780M (~27% margin)
- Gross margins mid-to-high 80s
- ~31,700 customers; Q3 added ~700 new logos (Q2 was 1,000+, strongest in years)
- Average contract duration ~3.4 years
- Public: NTNX

Healthy subscription business, not a turnaround.

## Competitive landscape

Main story is Broadcom/VMware. After Broadcom’s licensing and packaging changes, Nutanix is hunting disgruntled VMware accounts. CEO Rajiv Ramaswami has said they are targeting ~165K of VMware’s ~300K base over years, not overnight. Mid-market migrates faster; large enterprise often starts partial.

Also competes with Dell, Pure, Microsoft hybrid plays, Red Hat / Kubernetes stacks. Gartner has floated that a majority of enterprises may leave VMware for alternatives by ~2029 — Nutanix leans into that. Risk: Broadcom says most of VMware’s largest customers renewed. Opportunity is real; not guaranteed.

## Leadership

- CEO Rajiv Ramaswami (since Dec 2020) — ex VMware/Cisco-era operator; subscription transformation and profitability.
- Board chair Virginia Gambale (independent).
- CFO Rukmini Sivaraman.
- ~7–8K employees.

**Workforce:** 4 Aug 2026 they announced a ~5% global cut, mostly done by end of October; $33–43M severance charges. Stated reason: reallocate to AI, NKP, external storage, and customer-facing sales. Climate for a new design leader: growth company still pruning.

## Culture (public signal)

Mixed and team-dependent. Learning (Nutanix University) and technical craft show up as positives. Blind/AmbitionBox-style reviews also cite politics, uneven mid-level management (some India feedback), burnout in spots, promotion opacity. Interview the design org, not the brand page.

## Induprakas (Indu) Keri

SVP and GM, Hybrid Multicloud. Owns Nutanix Cloud Infrastructure (NCI — HCI core, described as $1B+ annual) and Nutanix Cloud Clusters (NC2). Previously Chief Product Security Officer at Nutanix. Before: COO Limelight, CTO SunGard, CISO Intuit; McKinsey, BEA, Oracle, SGI. PhD CS Cornell; IIT Madras.

Public voice: hybrid cloud + AI lifecycle (training vs inference vs control of data). Operator/GM, not a design leader. Likely a key stakeholder if design is meant to make the platform commercially clearer. Ask how he wants design to show up in NCI/NC2 buying and adoption.

## Satish Ramachandran (outgoing)

Long-time Global Head of Design (publicly in that seat since at least ~2014–2017; still hiring UX into 2025). Engineering first (Data Domain/EMC, Andiamo/Cisco, Tandem/HP), then design leadership. Org spans San Jose, Bengaluru, Berlin. Quiet, craft-and-simplicity orientation for hard infra UIs. Nutanix Design podcast / enterprise UX hiring.

Tam’s frame: engineer who became design leader. Replacement is meant to be a true design leader with a commercial mandate.

## Bottom line for Tam

Financially solid subscription company riding a real VMware-exit tailwind, investing in AI/Kubernetes while cutting ~5%. The open job is to succeed Satish and shift design from eng-craft to commercial viability. Get crystal clear on: reporting line, mandate vs. “keep the UI good,” relationship to Keri and product GMs, and how they will know the hire worked in 12–18 months.
