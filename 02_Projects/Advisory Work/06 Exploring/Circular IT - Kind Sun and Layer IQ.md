# Circular IT — Kind Sun and Layer IQ

Researched 2026-08-26. High-level briefing, not a dump. No numbers invented.

## Circular IT (the concept)

Linear IT: buy new, use, trash.
Circular IT: keep hardware in service, then reuse, repair, refurbish, remanufacture, recycle only what’s left.

The carbon win is mostly Scope 3 (embodied in *making* new kit). Cost is the other driver — 2026 memory/flash prices made life-extension and certified refurbished a procurement play, not just an ESG slide.

Stack, highest value down:
1. Extend life (third-party maintenance, spare parts)
2. Reuse / redeploy
3. Refurbish (clean, test, resell)
4. Remanufacture (tear down, replace worn parts, like-new warranty)
5. ITAD: wipe, recover value, recycle remainder

### Who plays

- **Hyperscale / enterprise ITAD:** Iron Mountain (bought ITRenew, 2022). Sims Lifecycle Services (8.8M assets refurbished for reuse in FY25). TES.
- **Data-center hardware still in the rack:** Evernex. Park Place and similar third-party maintainers.
- **Remanufactured laptops:** Circular Computing (UK).
- **OEM take-back:** Dell Asset Recovery, HPE Financial Services, Apple Trade In.
- **Consumer resale:** Back Market, Refurbed.
- **UK integrator example:** SCC (Circular IT + Recyclea).
- **Solar circular (adjacent, not IT):** SOLARCYCLE, Recycle PV Solar, We Recycle Solar.

## Kind Sun Initiative

Also called Solaris Americanus. They sometimes get referred to as Sunkind. [kindsun.org](https://kindsun.org/)

- NY 501(c)(3). EIN 33-1490678. NY Entity ID 7442809.
- Canadian not-for-profit, same name, same directors, incorporated 18 Nov 2024 (Toronto).
- Founder / ED: Mihail “Misha” Akopdjanov.
- COO: Drew Picklyk (Drew Sullivan Picklyk).
- Model: take used solar panels still at ~80% rated efficiency, refurbish, donate/install plus storage in energy-insecure communities.
- Started in Puerto Rico (public example: 15 kW + 14 kWh storage resilience center in Manatí). They say they’ve extended to Dominican Republic, Colombia, Panama.
- Unfit panels they won’t take; they point donors to recyclers.

This is circular *solar*, not circular IT. Same logic, different asset.

### Design / UX

No public design or UX capacity found. Leadership and Career footer links 404. About Us describes staff as nonprofit / labor / international development / fundraising / technology — no designer named. No open roles.

What exists digitally: a brochure site plus a recipient application form. Template leftovers (Jakarta address, `support@domain.tld`, generic Help Center / Ticket Support). Mission is real; the digital presence has not been designed.

**Read for Tam:** they need design help — brand, site, donor/recipient journey — the way a young nonprofit needs it. That is not the same as needing a design *leader* for a product org. They are not building software at scale. Capacity to pay for senior design is likely thin. Adjacent advisory or a short brand/site engagement is more plausible than a full seat.

## Layer IQ (formerly Terra Trust)

[layer-iq.com](https://www.layer-iq.com/) — old site [terratrust.ai](https://www.terratrust.ai/) still redirects.

- Layer IQ Technologies, Inc. Founded 2023. Port Orange / Daytona Beach, FL.
- CEO / founder: Mike DiPetrillo (ex FIÙTUR CTO; Devvio CRO / sustainability platforms).
- Also listed: Andrew Parets (CFO), Daniel Beveridge (CTO).
- Product: Asset IQ — software/intelligence layer on circular IT, not ITAD execution. Scores each asset (resale, parts, commodities, rare earths, carbon) and routes it: resell, refurbish, harvest, recycle.
- Pitch: the “circularity delta” — value lost when you retire kit without that routing. Buyer is ITADs first, then enterprises / OEMs / GHG teams.

### Business realities (what is actually knowable)

- Size: about 6 people. LinkedIn shows headcount down ~30% YoY. Real signal.
- Rebrand Terra Trust → Layer IQ (same people, tighter circular-IT story).
- Asset IQ v2 called out at ITAD Summit, July 2026.
- Data partnership with Rejoose announced May 2026 (ISAE 3000 / EY-audited LCA data into Asset IQ). Most concrete external validation.
- They say they’ve raised investment (Venture Connect, Mar 2026). Amount and investors not disclosed.
- Snowflake partnership mentioned under the Terra Trust brand. No public revenue. No named enterprise customers verified.

**Marketing vs evidence:** “$70B circularity gap,” “2.78x ROI,” and ITEC carbon instruments are *their* framing. Treat as pitch, not audited results.

**Stage:** early, small, selling into a real problem with a thin team and opaque finances. Risk is execution and concentration — if a few ITAD deals don’t land, a six-person shop feels it. Opportunity is sitting on the intelligence layer while Iron Mountain / Sims own the trucks.

## How the three relate

Kind Sun = reuse solar in the field.
Iron Mountain / Sims = move and process the boxes.
Layer IQ = decide what each box is worth and prove the carbon.
