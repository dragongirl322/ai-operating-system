- **Plan and blank templates:** this GitHub-backed Obsidian vault.

## Weekly review

[URI — weekly evidence & progress review](https://www.google.com/calendar/event?eid=YzYwcWlpMDRtMmJ1ZW8zNWE1NTI3b2k4azBfMjAyNjEwMjNUMjIwMDAwWiB0YW1zbm93QG0): Fridays, **3:00–3:30 p.m. America/Los_Angeles**, beginning October 23, 2026. The existing recurring series was retained; links to this guide and the templates were added.

1. Record significant issues, evidence, contradictions, alternative explanations, and decisions influenced.
2. Compare actual follow-up results with agreed baselines.
3. Identify patterns worth investigating and evidence that changed the diagnosis.
4. Check the advisor/operator boundary.
5. Set the next three moves; create or update linked Notion tasks.

At each weekly review, check the next substantive public idea against the plan's approximately 2–4 week publishing rhythm. Schedule the next concrete publication decision and date; the calendar review itself is not evidence of publication.

Where helpful, use trailing three months' realized revenue × 4 as a clearly labeled run-rate proxy; it does not prove repeatability. ## Reconciliation and status rules

Before adding a task, search the existing database by outcome and phase, including URI and Advisory workstreams. Reuse matching records even when wording differs. Preserve IDs, history, and actual status. Use **Doing** only when work has started, and **Done** only when linked evidence satisfies the task's completion criterion. If no client is secured or outcomes cannot yet be observed, record what remains unknown and keep the outcome open.

Do not attribute material to URI publicly without explicit permission. Do not turn pricing hypotheses into fixed prices, observations into causal proof, conversations into sales, or method drafts into demonstrated transfer.
