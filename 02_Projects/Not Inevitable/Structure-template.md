## Timing Map

| Time | Story job | Production note |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00-1:30 | Cold open: the hinge, just before it closes | No biography, housekeeping, or long theme music. |
| 1:30-4:00 | The known ending and the episode question | State the outcome without treating it as inevitable. |
| 4:00-18:00 | Perception: the world as it looked then | Make available information, uncertainty, and blind spots vivid. |
| 18:00-30:00 | Belief and action: how meaning became behavior | Show assumptions, authority, incentives, fear, and choices. |
| 30:00-39:00 | The System in the Room, when material | Show what tools, procedures, culture, workload, and authority enabled or constrained. |
| 39:00-45:00 | The hinge: when a different outcome was still possible | Test 1-3 plausible interventions against what was possible then. |
| 45:00-53:00 | Now That We Know | A clearly signaled tonal shift with your wife. |
| 53:00-55:00 | Carry-forward, sources, and uncertainty | Ask what this story helps us notice before the next hinge passes. |
