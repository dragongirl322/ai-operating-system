# Jason — Research Notes

_Notes for the book Jason asked to have written about him._

## Jason

- Born: May 14, 1971, in Ohio
- Parents: Terence Scott (father) and Margot (mother)

## Terence Scott (Jason's father)

### Marriages

- Married Margot ~1967
- Divorced Margot in 1983
- Married Patricia W. Twyford in January 1992
- Divorced Patricia in November 1996, in Harris, Texas
- Died March 2017, Salt Lake City, Utah

### Military Service

- Enlisted in the Army twice:
    - November 1957 – May 1958
    - April 1959 – April 1967
- Jason claimed his dad "blew a lung" and received an honorable discharge — unclear which enlistment this refers to

### Places Lived

- Salt Lake City, Utah
- West Valley, Utah
- Kirkland, Washington
- Houston, Texas
- Conroe, Texas
- Little Elm, Texas
- Akron, Ohio
- Austintown, Ohio

## Margot (Jason's mother)

- Born: May 1, 1949 — believed to have been born in Germany
- As a child, reportedly had syphilis injected into her head — reason unknown
- Two theories on origin: she may have contracted/been born with syphilis, or this connects to how she met Terence while he was serving in the military (unclear which, if either, is accurate)
- Lived in Elizabethtown, Kentucky, from 1993 to 2006
- Lost custody of Jason in 1982 — reason unknown
- After divorcing Terence (~1983), married Joseph L. Peace in November 1985 (Jason was 14 at the time)

## Boarding Schools / Custody Timeline

- 1982: Jason's first time in foster care, in Kentucky
- 1985: Jason in boarding school in Oneida, Kentucky — Baptist Institute (name as recorded in other notes; may need verification)
- Reference to a "TK Stone" school around the same time period — needs follow-up
- Also reportedly in foster care in Texas at some point, though no evidence/date found yet
- Jason says he was a runaway for a significant period
- Notes suggest Jason may have moved to Houston to be near his dad after Oneida, or was pulled to Houston by his dad
- Jason says he was sent to Oneida following a rape accusation; he claims he was a virgin at the time and did not understand how sex worked — unclear/unverified
- Possible juvenile record in Kentucky — worth chasing down

## Uncle Larry (Terence's brother)

- Jason claims Larry was a Jehovah's Witness who helped Terence build a JW church in Ohio
    - No record found (as of these notes) connecting any Twyfords to a JW church being built in Ohio — these remain Jason's claims, unverified
- Jason says Larry had him and his mother excommunicated from the JW religion after Margot's divorce (supposedly finalized in Brunswick, Ohio)
- Jason said Larry would be upset to know what happened to him
- Cousin **Butch** — believed to be Larry's son; Jason described him as a good cousin

## Terence ("Scott") — Behavior Jason Described as Mean

- Had Jason try to jump a creek; Jason couldn't make it and went underwater
- Was working on a radio, spliced the cords together, had Jason plug it in — burned Jason's thumb (Jason implies, without saying outright, that he later saw this as abuse)
- Pushed Jason away and was rude to him in front of his friends

## Terence ("Scott") — References to Abuse

- Punched Jason in the face at a Pizza Hut for pulling cheese off a pizza
- Made Jason smash his own Matchbox Honda car in 1974, when Jason was 5 or 6 — a car Jason loved
- When Jason brought his mom flowers, his dad would get angry and call them "weeds"
- Terence's last words to Jason were reportedly: "I forgot how evil you are."
- Burned Jason by the time Jason was 11 — no further detail known
- In Utah, threatened to shoot Jason

## Terence's Parents (Jason's grandparents)

- Father: Frank G. Twyford — died 1964, **before Jason was born**
- Mother: Ruth P. Twyford
- Lived in Austintown, Ohio, in 1950
- Had three children; Terence was the middle child

### Discrepancy to Note

Jason spoke of his grandfather (Frank) as having been head of the Jehovah's Witnesses and working on a church — but Frank died in 1964, seven years before Jason was born, so Jason could not have known this firsthand. Source of this claim is unclear and worth investigating further.
