My name is James Twitchell. I was born on May 11, 1971 in Cedarville, Ohio. I think I'm a troubled man. I don't sleep much. I have lived outside in the dark for a long, long time. Maybe it started when I was a kid. My dad was a truck driver. When he came home...I had peace when he was gone but then when he came home, well, he just….you could hear him outside and you knew the atmosphere was going to change. He always came home in the middle of the night.
