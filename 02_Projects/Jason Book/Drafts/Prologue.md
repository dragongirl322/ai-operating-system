**June 1985**

[L1] I woke with a start. My head throbbed. I felt disoriented and cold. Where was I? I slowly lifted my head, fighting nausea as I scanned the room. 

[L2] I remembered then. I was in the corner classroom of the old church they were renovating. I'd been here with Jennifer Weaver last night. She was older than me by four years. The last thing I remembered was her pouring more vodka in my cup and laughing. I knew I shouldn't be drinking, I was only fourteen. I didn't remember falling asleep here.

[L3] When the nausea passed, I sat up slowly. I looked at my hands and saw they were covered in blood. My shirt was covered in blood. What the hell? 

[L4] And then I saw her. 

[L5] In the corner, lying in a heap. I jumped up and hurried over to her, and saw immediately that she was naked from the waist down and covered in blood. I was sure she was dead but I didn't dare touch her. I was sick. I turned away from her and vomited. I'd pissed myself.  I didn't know what to do. 

[L6] I remembered there was a phone in the pastor's office, just down the hall. I ran as quickly as I could and picked up the receiver. I called home, praying that Stephen would answer. Dad would just beat me again if I called him and Mom had already had enough trouble with me. 

[L7] "Hello." It was Stephen! Thank God. 

[L8] "Stephen?" My voice was shaking. "Stephen. I need your help."
