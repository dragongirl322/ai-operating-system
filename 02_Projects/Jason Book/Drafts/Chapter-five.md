[L1] I was on the front room rug, playing with my Hot wheels. I could tell Mom was getting annoyed with me. "Jack," she said, "do you have to keep making those noises?" I stopped and got up and sat on the couch. I heard a knock on the door. Mom hurried over and answered it.
"You're early," she said. 
The lady answered, "Only by a few minutes. The drive took less time than I thought it would."
"Come in, have a seat," Mom said. 
The lady looked very proper. She was wearing a dress. Had neat dark brown, shoulder-length hair, and wore glasses. She was carrying a folder full of papers. She came in and sat on the couch. Mom walked into the kitchen, picked up a paper sack that was sitting on the kitchen table, and started toward my room. I followed her. 
"Mom, who's that lady? Where are you going?" No answer. Mom started putting pants, T-shirts, and underwear and socks from my drawers into the paper sack. "Mom, why are you putting my clothes in the sack? What's happening? Who is that lady?" No answer. 
Mom walked out of my bedroom, handed the sack to the lady. "Jack, you can't be here anymore. This is Miss Ashton, and she's going to take you to your new home." 
"Mom, why? Mom!” I started to cry. "I don’t want to go. I'll be good, I promise, I'll be good." I couldn't understand what I had done that would make my mom want to send me away with this strange, proper-looking lady. 
The lady took my hand and started to walk me toward the door. I pulled away from her and ran to my mom and clutched her, screaming, "Mom, Mom, don't make me go, Mom! I promise I'll be good, I promise I'll be good." 
She grabbed me by the shoulders and pushed me away and said, "Jack, go with Miss Ashton, be a good boy." I was crying, and I wanted to fight, but I knew there was no point. I let Miss Ashton take me by the hand and lead me toward the door. I dared look back one more time and saw Stephen peeking around from the hallway. I thought Mom might feel something, might say something, might at least say goodbye or give me a hug, but nothing.  She just looked at me with no expression.

[L2] Miss Ashton opened the back door and got me seated, and then walked to the driver's side front door, got in, placed the paper sack on the passenger seat next to her. "Where are you taking me?" I asked meekly through my tears. 
"I'm taking you to your new family. I think you'll like them." I started crying more, but kept it quiet because I know good little boys don't cry.

[L3] Miss Ashton drove for what seemed like a very long time, and I pulled into the driveway in front of a small pink house. "Where are we?" I asked. 
"We're in a town called Grant's Pass," she answered. 
She climbed out of the front seat with the paper sack in her hands, opened the back door for me, took me by the hand, and led me to the front door and rang the bell. 
A nice-looking man opened the door. He was tall, a little taller than my dad, but then with dark hair and glasses. He had a really cool horse tattoo on his left arm. A lady came up behind him. She had long blonde hair and a pleasant look. 
"You must be Jack," she said, the friendly tone of her voice. "We're the Mercers. We're going to be your foster parents."
