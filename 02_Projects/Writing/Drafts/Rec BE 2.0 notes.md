# Rec #1 — BE 2.0 (Jim Collins and Bill Lazier)

Captured 2026-09-06. For Substack Tue Sep 8.

## How I found him

First introduced via Brené Brown's podcast. What landed: he is a grounded theory researcher, and the way he talks about creating a conceptual vessel for insights.

## Three things I like about the book

1. **Best of any book I've read for business principles and frameworks** — from learning how to catalyze a vision and accompanying strategy to becoming a better leader.
2. **Full of great stories** that make the concepts much easier to understand and apply; he demonstrates the conceptual vessel throughout.
3. **Full of humanity** — especially the way he speaks of Bill Lazier, his mentor and father figure.

## Top lessons I took from it

- The hedgehog principle
- Level 5 leadership
- Never stifle a generous impulse

## Stance for the post

Don't be the wise one; help people find their own wisdom.
