It was spring of 2020 and I was working from home during the day and, like everybody else, stuck at home with my thoughts when I wasn't working. My wife was in the middle of chemo treatment for stage IV cancer. To keep myself grounded and occupied, I'd sit at my kitchen table coloring while listening to Brene Brown's *Dare to Lead* podcast through my earbuds. One of my favorite episodes was her interview with Jim Collins, author of several great leadership books including "Good to Great." I was immediately drawn to his ideas because, like Brene, he is a grounded theory researcher and as a researcher myself, I could relate to the conversation. They talked about creating a conceptual vessel for insights, *The Stockdale Paradox*, leadership, and his book, BE 2.0, a new and improved version of a book he and Bill Lazier had written many years previously. I immediately ordered the book and read it shortly after it arrived. It is one of the best leadership books I've read and definitely the best in terms of understanding business context. The top three things concepts I took away from the book are the hedgehog principle, level 5 leadership, and to never stifle a generous impulse.
