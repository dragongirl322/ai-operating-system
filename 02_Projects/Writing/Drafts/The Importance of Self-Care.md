A little over 3 years ago, I wrote a set of four rules on the white board that sits in my mud room. It is a board I look at several times a day and I wanted these rules in a place where I'd be constantly reminded. The rules:

Good sleep
Good food
Move & get fresh air
Lower stress

We were just coming out of covid. My wife had gone through chemo, radiation, and surgeries for stage 4 cancer through 2020. I had taken on a leadership role with huge scope and a large, multi-discipline team. I loved the work enough that I was putting in significant hours every day and rarely taking time off. My body was telling me that my central nervous system was on overload. These are the four non-negotiable rules I set for myself.
