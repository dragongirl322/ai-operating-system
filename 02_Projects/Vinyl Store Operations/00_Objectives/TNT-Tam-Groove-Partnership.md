# TNT Music — Tam & Groove Partnership

**Status:** Plan of record (finalized 2026-08-24)

How Tam and Groove run the business together. Emma runs the floor. Tre leads inventory (with Tam's input). We stay out of their lanes unless they ask.

Informed by Year Two growth strategy and a "learn from the best" study of Easy Street Records (West Seattle), Music Millennium (Portland), and The Next Record Store (Santa Rosa). Steal mechanisms that fit Edmonds scale; skip cafe empires, celebrity in-stores, and 100-show calendars. Detail: `03_Marketing/TNT-Learn-From-The-Best.md`.

## One-line charter

> Tam and Groove grow TNT through systems, local discovery, content, owned audience, and events — while Emma runs the room and Tre runs the stock.

## Team map

| Who | Lane | Owns day-to-day |
|---|---|---|
| **Emma** | Store associate / floor | In-store customer experience, opening/closing rhythm, floor standards, asking customers into Groove Society at checkout, hosting events on the floor, executing the weekly ops checklist |
| **Tre** | Inventory lead | Buying, reorders, stock levels, staples vs discovery mix, RAIN→Shopify inventory accuracy, stock for themed drops / listening-party titles (with Tam input on budget and curation). Weekly reorder queue comes from **Vinyl Scout** (Tam runs it; Tre places the orders). |
| **Tam** | Owner / admin / strategy | Decisions, money, partnerships, brand, approvals, Shopify cutover go/no-go, growth targets |
| **Groove** | Biz partner (ops + growth) | Planning, drafting, systems, tracking, marketing execution after approval, making Mondays productive |

**Inventory truth:** Vinyl Scout is the weekly reorder source of truth. Tam downloads two RAIN inventory files, uploads them to Vinyl Scout, prints the output, and Tre places the orders. Groove does not invent a parallel reorder process.

**Rule:** Floor and inventory questions go to Emma and Tre first. Tam + Groove work the systems that grow the business around them.

## What Tam + Groove own together

Aligned to the Year Two growth system (~$4K/mo baseline → staged climb toward ~$7K/mo by Q4 2027; ~$250/mo marketing budget; Sept 24 anniversary as a brand milestone).

Paid ads are seasoning. Events, partners, email, and Google are the meal.

### 1. Customer capture & Groove Society
- **Outcome:** Every willing customer is findable in Shopify; Groove Society enrollment is a habit, not a project.
- **We do:** Design the checkout script, Shopify customer fields/flow, staff one-pagers, dry-run checklist (target Aug 25), email list hygiene.
- **Emma does:** Deliver the ask at the register.
- **Tre does:** N/A (unless inventory tools touch customer history later).

### 2. Local discovery (Google + Apple + Maps + tourism)
- **Outcome:** When someone nearby searches vinyl / record store / gift ideas, TNT shows up complete, current, and active.
- **We do:** GBP description, attributes, photos cadence, 1–2 Google posts/week, review replies within 48 hrs (draft → Tam OK → post), Visit Edmonds / tourism listing, Apple Business Connect if/when useful.
- **Emma / Tre:** Feed us real moments (new bins, events, sold-out staples) and ask happy customers for reviews when it fits.

### 3. Content engine (IG, Facebook, Google)
- **Outcome:** Steady traffic-driving content without Tam living in Canva every day. Social announces; the store delivers the experience.
- **We do:** Weekly plan, draft copy/graphics direction, post on Tam's approval, biweekly metrics + suggested fixes (then implement only with permission). Tease arrivals on IG; keep hottest used in-store only when that drives visits.
- **Emma / Tre:** Occasional phone pics and staff-pick notes; Emma may appear in content with consent.

### 4. Email / owned audience
- **Outcome:** Owned list that drives return visits (Shopify Email first; Klaviyo later only if needed). Email-first calendar: every event + "new this week."
- **We do:** Welcome + monthly/biweekly cadence, Spinlist-style playlists, event RSVPs, anniversary sequences, occasional enter-to-win for list growth.
- **Emma:** Capture emails via Groove Society / checkout.

### 5. Events, local press & partnerships
- **Outcome:** The store is a third place, not just a retail box. One repeatable low-lift monthly anchor + plaza/Chamber/café partnerships.
- **Constraint:** No live music / no alcohol unless Tam explicitly changes that. Prefer listening parties, trivia, family days, plaza pop-ups, purchase-required meet-and-greets only if live sets are allowed.
- **90-day event pattern (peer-informed):**
  1. Monthly free listening party (IG RSVP, one raffle)
  2. Hyperlocal press habit — My Edmonds News / Beacon tip + 2 photos per event
  3. One plaza / café partner ritual per quarter (coffee for early RSD line; pastry day; shared posts)
  4. One signature annual day near Sept 24 (scaled Customer Appreciation — partners, prize wheel with merchant coupons)
- **We do:** Calendar, promos, partner outreach drafts, press tips, measurement (turnout, repeat visitors).
- **Emma:** Host/floor for events. **Tre:** Stock for themed drops / listening-party titles.

### 6. Online sales pilot (after POS is stable)
- **Outcome:** 60–90 day selected-product pilot (new vinyl, select CDs, gifts, accessories, bundles) — not a full used-catalog dump. Discogs or light catalog for reach; hottest used stays in-store.
- **We do:** Scope SKUs with Tre/Tam, theme, fulfillment rules, landing/pages, promo.
- **Tre:** Inventory truth. **Emma:** Pickup / customer questions in-store.

### 7. Shopify cutover & store systems
- **Outcome:** Hardware works, Online Store matches reality, RAIN sunset without losing history, website (tntmusic.shop) doesn't fight Shopify.
- **We do:** Checklists, test plans, website/content alignment, issue log, go-live criteria.
- **Tre:** Inventory migration accuracy. **Emma:** Register workflow validation.

### 8. Measurement & plan of record
- **Outcome:** This document + Growth Tracker as execution source of truth.
- **We do:** Monday reviews, weekly "what matters," monthly revenue vs target, adjust Year Two plan without rewriting strategy every week.
- **Sources:** Year Two Google Doc, Growth Tracker sheet, `2026 Objectives.md`, `TNT-Learn-From-The-Best.md`.

## What we explicitly do *not* own

- Day-to-day floor coverage and customer service style → **Emma**
- What to buy / how deep to stock a title → **Tre** (Tam input on budget/curation)
- Publishing, spending, or changing live brand/settings without Tam's OK → **never Groove alone**
- Cafe/bar P&L, celebrity in-store pipelines, 100+ events/year, own-label/radio empires → **out of scope** (wrong scale)

## Operating rhythm (Tam + Groove)

|---|---|

| **Weekly** | Content live; GBP posts; press tips if an event happened; tracker updates; blockers surfaced |
| **Biweekly** | Social + Google metrics review → improvement proposals |
| **Monthly** | Listening party (or equivalent anchor); revenue vs target; email performance |
| **Quarterly** | Café/plaza partner ritual; partnership scorecard |

## ~$250/mo marketing sketch

- ~$80 local social boosts (peak weeks)
- ~$50 prizes / raffle product
- ~$40 partner hospitality
- ~$30 Discogs / shipping buffer
- $0–15 email tool

## Outcomes we judge ourselves on

Leading indicators (not vanity alone):
1. Groove Society / Shopify customer capture rate
2. Google profile completeness + post consistency + review response time
3. Content shipped vs planned (and engagement quality)
4. Email list growth + open/click that drives visits
5. Event turnout and repeat visit signals
6. Progress on Shopify cutover milestones
7. Revenue run-rate vs staged Year Two targets

## Decision rights

- **Tam decides:** money, partnerships, go-live, brand exceptions, anything public-facing final.
- **Groove recommends:** prioritization, drafts, checklists, "do these three next."
- **Silence ≠ approval** for publishing or live changes.
