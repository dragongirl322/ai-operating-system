# 2026 Objectives

## Objectives

- Reach $12,000 average monthly sales by end of year
- Win "Best Of Edmonds" Music Store category

---

## Success Definition

### Revenue Targets (Run-Rate)

| Quarter | Target | Actual |
|---------|--------|--------|
| Q1 | $8K/month | $5,768/month |
| Q2 | $8K/month | — |
| Q3 | $10K/month | — |
| Q4 | $12K/month | — |

### What Must Be True to Hit $12K/Month

- Store awareness increases
- Weekly in-store traffic is consistent
- Repeat customers increase month-over-month
- Core titles stay in stock (no "sold-out staples")
- Inventory cash isn't trapped in dead stock

## Operating Cadence

### Every Transaction

- Ask every customer to join Groove Society
- If customer is in Groove Society, associate the sale to their account

### 2x a Week

- Capture at least 1 photo or short clip for social and post

### Every Week

- Generate Reorder List + place orders
- Micro-events: launch anniversary, concert, new arrival
- Keep "Staff Picks" / "New Arrivals" areas fresh
- Store maintenance (alternating):
  - Week A: clean floors and bathroom
  - Week B: dust and windows

### Every Month

- Create report on revenue performance for month
- Send newsletter to subscribers

### Every Quarter

- Update tax tracker
- Review revenue vs. quarterly target

---

## Inventory Strategy

### Reorder Rules

- Use vinyl-scout

### Dead Stock Reset

- Items aged 60+ days, low sell-through → move to feature / better placement
- Items aged 90+ days, 0 sales → markdown / bundle / remove from prime space

### Inventory Budget

> Until data says otherwise

- 70% proven sellers + staples
- 20% special orders
- 10% curated discovery (breadth over depth)

## Marketing Strategy

### Google Business Profile (Highest Leverage)

- Post weekly update (new arrivals / event / hours)
- Add fresh photos weekly
- Respond to every review within 24–48 hrs

### Social (Traffic-Driving Content)

3 posts/week:

- 1 reel: arrivals flip / "3 you need"
- 1 story: staff pick w/ quick narrative
- 1 post: event promo / community highlight

### Local Awareness Accelerators

- 2 partnerships per quarter (restaurants, barber, coffee, brewery)
- Quarterly local PR push (paper / blogs / community pages)
- TNT posters/flyers in community
