# TNT new-release buy criteria


> Do not score with this file. Tam is sending the actual buy rules she already uses. Replace this document when they arrive.

Use this when Groove scores **upcoming and new releases** so Tam knows what to invest in.

Do **not** use this for weekly restock. Weekly reorder is Vinyl Scout: Tam downloads two RAIN files, Vinyl Scout applies her reorder rules, Tre places that order.

## Mix (until data says otherwise)

From `2026 Objectives.md`:

- 70% proven sellers + staples
- 20% special orders
- 10% curated discovery (breadth over depth)

A new-release buy has to name which bucket it is. Discovery should stay thin (usually 1 copy).

## Who it is for

Millennial vinyl lovers roughly 30–45 in Edmonds / Snohomish / North King. People who want to spin their music. First-timers and collectors both fit around that.

Genres we already claim: rock, pop, jazz, country, soundtracks. Local / Sub Pop / Seattle scene can punch above their weight.

## Score each title

For every title, answer:

1. **Fit.** Does this person walk into TNT and feel seen? If it is only a collector-flex or a format we do not sell, skip.
2. **Bucket.** Staple, special order, or discovery. If you cannot name the bucket, skip.
3. **Already covered?** If Vinyl Scout would restock this from RAIN, do not double-buy it here.
4. **Sit risk.** Would this still be here at 60 days? At 90 it is dead-stock territory. High cost + unknown name + no local story = skip or 1 copy max.
5. **Why us.** One reason: staple gap, gift, local, staff-pick energy, or a real preorder. "It exists" is not a reason.
6. **Qty.** Discovery 1. Special order only if someone asked. Staple: 2–3 unless Tam/Tre say deeper.
7. **Call.** Buy / skip / ask Tre.

## What Groove returns

A short list, not a novel:

- **Buy now** (title, bucket, qty, one-line why)
- **Ask Tre** (only if the call is actually close)
- **Skip** (one line, grouped)

No invented stock, no invented costs. If URP/RAIN cost or on-hand is missing, say so.

## Sources Tam can drop in

- URP latest / new-release sheets
- Label or distributor one-stops
- A pasted list of upcoming titles

Groove does not log into Shopify or RAIN unless Tam asks.
