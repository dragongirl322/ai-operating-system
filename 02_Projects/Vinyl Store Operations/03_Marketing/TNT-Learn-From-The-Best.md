# Learn from the Best

Sam Walton method: study the best, steal mechanisms that fit our size, skip what needs their scale.

**Constraint reminder:** Year Two plan assumes no live music / no alcohol events unless Tam changes that. Prefer listening parties, trivia, family days, plaza pop-ups, purchase-required meet-and-greets only if/when live sets are allowed.

## Peer snapshot

| Store | Why we studied them | Engine in one line |
|---|---|---|
| **Easy Street** (West Seattle) | Regional gold standard; neighborhood + national clout | Buy-the-album events + local press + hospitality rituals |
| **Music Millennium** (Portland) | PNW institution; modular community tactics | Signature annual day + weekly email + kids/family + CIMS/RSD heritage |
| **The Next Record Store** (Santa Rosa) | Closest market scale (mid-size NorCal city) | Listening parties + brewery collab + tourism listing + social-first |

## Shared patterns (what great indies actually do)

1. Events *are* the brand (free, frequent enough, low ticket)
2. Hyperlocal partners beat paid media
3. RSD is the annual peak + hospitality
4. Social + email announce; store delivers the experience
5. Hybrid commerce: site/Discogs for reach; sometimes keep best used in-store only
6. Anti-chain story with a clear local slogan
7. Contests grow the list
8. Staff personality > SKU count

Paid ads are seasoning, not the meal.

## Steal for TNT (~$250/mo)

**Do next (90 days)**
1. **Monthly free listening party** (Next model) — IG RSVP, one raffle, label posters when available
2. **Hyperlocal press habit** (Easy Street → WSB) — My Edmonds News / Beacon tip + 2 photos per event
3. **One plaza / café partner ritual** per quarter (coffee for early RSD line; pastry day; shared posts) — skip brewery if alcohol constraint holds
4. **Email-first calendar** — every event + "new this week"; capture at POS via Groove Society
5. **Google Business discipline** — weekly post, fresh photos, reply to every review in 48 hrs
6. **Tease arrivals on IG; keep hottest used in-store only** (Millennium) to drive visits
7. **Tourism / Visit Edmonds listing** (Next → Sonoma Tourism)
8. **One signature annual day** near Sept 23 anniversary (scaled Customer Appreciation — partners bring food, prize wheel with merchant coupons)

**Budget sketch/mo:** ~$80 local boosts + ~$50 prizes + ~$40 partner hospitality + ~$30 Discogs/ship buffer + $0–15 email ≈ $250

## Do NOT copy (scale mismatch)

- Cafe / bar / brunch P&L (Easy Street)
- Celebrity / national tour-stop pipeline
- 100+ in-stores/year production machine (Millennium)
- Own label / radio show empire (Next's full stack) — start with guesting on local podcasts/radio instead
- Van delivery brand theater
