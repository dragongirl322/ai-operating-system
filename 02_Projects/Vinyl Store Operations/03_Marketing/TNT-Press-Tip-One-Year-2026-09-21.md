**TNT Music celebrates one year in Edmonds**

Edmonds vinyl shop TNT Music is marking one year of being open with a customer-appreciation day on **Thursday, September 24, from 11 a.m. to 7 p.m.**

The store at **8416 Bowdoin Way** (Five Corners Plaza) is owned by **Tre**, a **22-year-old Edmonds native**. The shop opened around September 2025 so he could build a modern, welcoming record store for the community — curated pressings, expert guidance, and a chill place to put a record on and take your time.

On the one-year day, shoppers can expect:

- A **free gift with any purchase** from a free bin
- **Tre’s staff-favorites stack** from stock
- The **final day of the store’s raffle**, which has been running since early September — buy an album for a ticket; post a purchase and tag TNT for five tickets. Prize is a red turntable or a boombox, plus two albums from the floor. Draw is at the store on the 24th.

Regular hours the rest of the week are **Tuesday–Saturday, 11–7**. TNT Music is also online at **tntmusic.shop**.
