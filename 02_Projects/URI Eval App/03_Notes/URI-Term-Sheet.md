## 1. Role and Decision Authority

I take this on as the design and AI experience lead for the implementation pillar. Authority has to match accountability, or the outcomes are someone else's to block.

**I own outright:**
- Research and design strategy for the pillar
- The method, curriculum, and quality bar for the workshops
- Go / no-go on whether a solution is ready to ship against the experience standard

**I share, as lead voice:**
- Which AI initiatives are worth pursuing
- Hiring and shaping any design or research talent the pillar needs

**I advise:**
- Commercial packaging and client selection

If URI wants the outcomes I'm describing, I need the authority in the first list held firm. That's the line I don't negotiate away.

## 3. Intellectual Property

This is the part that matters most, so it's the most specific.

**What's mine stays mine.** I own the frameworks, the method, the curriculum, and the measurement architecture. The established foundations underneath (the field's published guidelines) are credited and belong to no one. What URI pays for is the synthesis, the sequence, and the decision engine I built on top. Those are the licensable asset.

**License to deliver the workshops:**
- **Structure:** upfront license fee plus ongoing royalty. Never a buyout.
- **Upfront fee:** $40,000–$80,000, sized to how much build URI wants. This pays for productizing the material. It does not transfer ownership.
- **Royalty:** 20%–35% of delivered workshop revenue, or a fixed per-seat / per-delivery fee where the accounting is cleaner.
- **Certification:** I certify URI facilitators. Paid per facilitator, with annual re-certification that keeps the material current.

**Four terms I hold:**
1. **Non-exclusive, field-limited.** URI uses it inside its own client base. I keep every right to use and license it elsewhere, including in my own practice.
2. **I own all derivatives.** Anything we build together defaults to me, defined up front.
3. **My name travels with it.** The frameworks carry my attribution inside URI's delivery.
4. **It reverts on exit.** Engagement ends, license ends, materials return, URI stops using my named methods.

## 4. Payment Terms

- Retainer: monthly, in advance.
- Workshops: 50% on booking, balance on delivery.
- Projects and license fees: 50% up front, 50% on completion. Net 30.
- Royalties: reported and paid quarterly.

## 5. Term, Renewal, and Exit

- Fractional term: 6 months, renewable by mutual agreement.
- License term: 12 months, auto-renewing, either side can exit with 60 days' notice.
- On exit, Section 3 governs. Nothing about the wind-down is left to goodwill.

## 6. Exclusivity

None, by default. If URI wants exclusivity, it's a premium and it's time-boxed. Perpetual exclusivity is off the table. It would cost me the thing I'm building.
