# FDE partnership outreach — one-pager

Working note for outreach to smaller forward-deployed engineering (FDE) firms. Peer set to reFWD AI. Goal: explore if/how URI partners — research + human evidence + discovery alongside their embed-and-ship engineering.

---

## Elevator pitch — URI’s unique value

URI is a human-evidence partner for AI that has to work in the real world. We bring the people lens that most FDE engagements skip: discovery that surfaces user outcomes and behaviors before anyone builds, plus panel, HITL evaluation, and adoption evidence after systems ship. You embed and build production AI in the client’s environment; we make sure the right problem is being solved for real humans — and that what ships is trusted, usable, and adopted.

**Where we play with an FDE partner**

1. **Discovery (human lens)** — Before/alongside scoping: user outcomes, behaviors, jobs-to-be-done, failure modes, and what “good” looks like for the people who will use or be affected by the system.
2. **Build-time evidence** — Participants, tasks, and human judgment loops while agents/tools are being built (not only after).
3. **Eval & readiness** — Independent human evaluation of whether the AI is correct, understandable, controllable, and ready for real workflows.
4. **Adoption & continuous improvement** — Evidence that follows the lifecycle: decision → change → retest — so production systems keep earning trust.

**One-liner for outreach:** *You ship the last mile in their repo. We make sure the first mile (and the humans) aren’t missing.*

## Top 5 firms to reach out to

### 1. Forward Deployed — [forwardeployed.com](https://www.forwardeployed.com/)
- **Who:** Boutique FDE focused on PE portfolios / operating teams; 30/60/90 workflow → production agent with eval + governance + operator training.
- **Why URI:** Strongest eval/governance language in the peer set; natural fit for human evidence and “does this survive real work?”
- **Outreach angle:** Complement their scorecard/evals with independent human evidence + discovery so workflow selection isn’t only ops-metric driven.
- **First-call asks:** How do you pick the “one workflow”? Who defines success for end users vs operators? Do clients ever ask for human eval / adoption research you don’t staff?

### 2. Forward Labs — [forwardlabsai.com](https://forwardlabsai.com/)
- **Who:** Independent FDE (SF + Istanbul); model-agnostic; ~4 weeks diagnosis on real data, ~90 days to production; sovereignty/compliance-aware.
- **Why URI:** Clear “not slideware” posture; diagnosis phase is an opening for human-lens discovery.
- **Outreach angle:** Pair their technical diagnosis with URI discovery on user outcomes/behaviors so the first use case is both operationally valuable and human-validated.
- **First-call asks:** What happens in the 4-week diagnosis today? Who talks to end users? Where have deployments stalled on adoption or trust after tech shipped?

### 3. Phiva AI — [phiva.ai](https://phiva.ai/) (Atlanta)
- **Who:** “FDE as a service” / white-label FDE for startups and teams that can’t hire FDE in-house; 6-week sprint framing.
- **Why URI:** Channel potential — they may want a research/eval partner when clients need human evidence without building that capability.
- **Outreach angle:** Offer URI as the human-evidence module they can bring into FDE engagements (discovery + HITL eval), white-label or co-branded.
- **First-call asks:** Do you ever need discovery/research you don’t deliver yourselves? How do you prove ROI beyond “it runs”? Interest in a packaged research + FDE offer?

### 4. FDE Team — [fdeteam.com](https://www.fdeteam.com/ai-implementation-partner)
- **Who:** Mid-market AI implementation pods; embed in client stack; weeks to first production deploy; outcome-accountable framing.
- **Why URI:** Mid-market clients often lack internal UX/research; URI fills that gap without slowing their ship cadence.
- **Outreach angle:** Joint motion — URI discovery sprint → their build pod → URI eval/adoption check before scale.
- **First-call asks:** Typical client research maturity? Willingness to insert a short discovery/eval gate? Who owns “user success” vs “system live”?

### 5. Forward Deployed Engineers (agency) — [forwarddeployedengineers.agency](https://forwarddeployedengineers.agency/)
- **Who:** Very small / fixed-fee 6-week embeds; published price bands; production workflow build + handoff.
- **Why URI:** Approachable; may partner faster than larger shops; fixed scope makes a URI discovery/eval add-on easy to price.
- **Outreach angle:** Bundle — URI human-lens discovery + their 6-week production build + URI readiness eval at handoff.
- **First-call asks:** How do you scope which workflow to build? Ever lost a deal because buyer wanted research/eval you don’t offer? Open to a referral or co-sell package?

**Also on deck (not top 5):** Fifty One Degrees (UK/US, regulated industries), fdeai.agency, FluentData FDE, ForwardAI (solo/small), METECH / FDE Taiwan / tessor (APAC).

## Outreach notes (keep light)

- Lead with complementarity, not “we also build.” They own embed/ship; we own human evidence and discovery.
- Name the gap: FDE wins on production code; AI still fails on wrong problem, weak trust, or no adoption — that’s our lane.
- Ask for one recent engagement story where tech shipped but humans struggled (or discovery was thin).
- Offer a concrete next step: 30-min mapping call, or a joint one-pager for a shared target account.
- Include reFWD AI in the same outreach wave (existing relationship) — use as learning for what partnership could look like.

---

## Suggested first-call arc (~20–30 min)

1. Their model in one minute (who they embed with, typical engagement length, what “done” means).
2. URI pitch (above) — stress **discovery with a human lens** + eval/adoption, not only post-hoc testing.
3. Where overlap vs handoff lives (discovery → build → eval → adopt).
4. One hypothetical joint engagement (pick their ICP).
5. Decide: explore further / intro to URI leadership / park.
