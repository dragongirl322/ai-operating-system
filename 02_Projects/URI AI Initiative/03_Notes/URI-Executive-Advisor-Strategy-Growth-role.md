# Executive Advisor, Strategy & Growth

Advisor to the CEO · User Research International (URI)
Role description · Tammy Snow

**Partners with**
Trinh, CEO. Primary partnership is with the CEO. Work also spans growth, client leadership, and other leaders as needed to execute strategy.

**Engagement model**
Executive advisor to the CEO. Hands-on enough to drive execution. Does not replace line ownership.

**Purpose**
Increase URI’s strategic clarity, growth quality, and execution capacity. Help define where URI will play and how it will win, then help the company follow through. Grow the business by strengthening existing client relationships, refining go-to-market, and building leadership effectiveness. AI is treated as core to URI’s growth strategy, not as a separate initiative or business unit.

This role is designed to create enterprise value: clearer strategy, stronger retention and expansion, more disciplined GTM, and a credible AI-era commercial agenda that a buyer can diligence.

---

## Primary responsibilities

1. **Advisor to the CEO**
   Trusted counsel to Trinh on company direction, priorities, and difficult calls. Available for what she needs. Also proactive: identify opportunities for impact, surface risks early, and bring recommendations, not only respond to assignments.

2. **Strategy definition and execution**
   Partner with Trinh and the leadership team to clarify ICP, offer priorities, where URI will compete, and what it will stop doing. Translate strategy into priorities, owners, milestones, and operating rhythm. Stay involved through execution reviews, not only through framing.

3. **Growth strategy (including AI)**
   Shape and advance one integrated growth agenda across:
   - Existing client satisfaction, retention, and expansion
   - New business growth
   - Go-to-market refinement (offer clarity, buyer focus, messaging, packaging, and sales motion)
   - AI-era positioning and offers as part of core growth strategy, not as a parallel track

4. **Full funnel: pipeline through GTM through delivery**
   As part of strategy work, play a clear role in defining and supporting execution across the full funnel: creating pipeline, shaping GTM strategy, and building and delivering. Stay connected from opportunity creation through how offers are sold and how work is delivered, so strategy does not stop at slides.

5. **Client relations and advisory consulting**
   Engage selectively with key clients and prospects to strengthen trust, deepen insight, and support retention and expansion where it advances strategy. Define and lead advisory consulting activities with clients on behalf of URI. Train the trainer by bringing URI employees along on that work so capability grows inside the firm, not only through the advisor.

6. **Innovation (mission-aligned)**
   Play a role in identifying and driving innovations when they align to URI’s mission. Surface and advance opportunities that strengthen the core or open credible growth paths; do not chase innovation for its own sake or as a separate AI lab.

7. **Leadership mentoring and guidance**
   Coach and guide leaders and teams so strategy can be executed well. Provide people-leadership coaching and workshop facilitation when they directly support strategy, growth, or GTM execution.

---

## Decision rights

- Tam recommends, frames options, and helps drive execution.
- Trinh decides material company direction, investment, and priority tradeoffs.
- Line leaders own day-to-day delivery, sales execution, and team management.
- Tam does not replace the CEO, sales leadership, or delivery leadership.

---

## Out of scope (unless the CEO explicitly expands it)

- Standing up a separate AI business unit, lab, or HITL labor marketplace as a default mandate
- Owning the full sales P&L or acting as de facto CRO
- Permanent people-management of URI staff as a substitute for line leadership

---

## Success measures (value-creation outcomes)

Success is judged by progress a PE buyer would recognize, including:

- A written, shared company strategy with clear priorities and stop-doing choices
- An integrated growth plan that includes retention/expansion, new business, and GTM, with AI embedded in the core agenda
- Measurable improvement in client health for priority accounts (satisfaction, retention, expansion pipeline)
- Clearer ICP, offer hierarchy, and sales narrative used consistently by leadership and sales
- Operating cadence that turns strategy into decisions and follow-through (owners, milestones, reviews)
- Full-funnel coherence: pipeline creation, GTM, and delivery reinforce the same strategy
- Advisory consulting that lands with clients and transfers capability to URI employees
- Mission-aligned innovation that advances strategy rather than diluting focus
- Leadership effectiveness on strategy-critical work (clarity, accountability, reduced thrash)
- External story coherence: URI is positioned as a growth company with a disciplined AI-era offer, not as a firm running a side AI project

Exact targets and baselines to be set with Trinh (and finance/sales as needed) for each planning period.

---

## Additional support (as requested)

- People leadership coaching
- Workshop facilitation
- Other CEO-directed priorities

These are available and valuable. They are enablers of strategy and growth, not the center of the role.

---

## Working model

- Mandate comes from the CEO, and Tam also identifies opportunities for impact and brings forward suggestions and recommendations
- Work is concentrated on strategy, growth (including AI), full-funnel GTM and delivery support, client advisory consulting with train-the-trainer, mission-aligned innovation, and leadership guidance
- Collaboration with sales, delivery, and innovation leaders is expected; accountability for company decisions remains with the CEO
- AI-related work is governed through growth strategy and operating priorities, not as a freestanding entity

---

## How this role strengthens URI for investors

- Reduces key-person overload on the CEO by adding senior strategy and growth capacity
- Improves transferability: clearer strategy, GTM, and operating rhythm are easier to diligence and scale
- Ties AI to commercial outcomes rather than to an unscoped initiative
- Strengthens retention and expansion, not only net-new logos
- Builds internal advisory and delivery capability through train-the-trainer, not advisor dependency alone
- Keeps authority clean: advisor-driven value creation without governance confusion
