Discussion doc for Tam and Shawn. Not a final org design. Goal: align on how we install a real GTM motion, and carve a dignified role for Justin that uses what people value in him without letting him scramble the system.

Related: `URI GTM strategy draft.md`

---

## 1. Why we’re talking

- URI does not have a true GTM strategy. Tactics are ad hoc. Pipeline is thinning. Growth is stalled.
- The one sales person moves under Shawn in **November**. That is also when Trinh wants to **roll out FY27 strategy and goals**. GTM install and FY27 launch are the same moment, not two tracks.
- Sales under Shawn only works if he owns a **system** (ICP, qualification, cadence), not order-taking from whoever yelled last.
- Justin is repeatedly described as maverick / dreamer / willing to make first connections. He is also disruptive: speed-as-urgency, low visibility to Trinh, toxically optimistic with staff, opaque “innovation” motion with Dalien.
- Trinh needs him out of the critical path of Sales and day-to-day delivery control, without a public demotion that blows up founder dignity.

**Success for this conversation:** Shawn leaves clear on what he owns Nov 1, what “good” looks like in Q4/Q1, and how Justin is allowed to help without owning pipeline or creating shadow work.

## 5. Proposed role for Justin: “Founder Network & Market Catalyst”

Working title options (pick language Trinh can live with):
- Founder, Market Catalyst
- Founder, Partnerships & First Conversations
- Founder, Opportunity Scout (non-pipeline owner)

### Mandate (one sentence)
Justin opens doors, surfaces early market conversations, and brings founder-level introductions into a **single intake path owned by Shawn**, with full visibility to Trinh.

### In scope
1. **First conversations** with prospective clients, partners, and interesting market contacts (especially warm network).
2. **Partnership scouting** (e.g. FDE firms, agencies, PE ops) as intros, not commercial ownership.
3. **Market sensing** notes: what he’s hearing about buyer pain, AI-era research demand, competitive noise (written, short, weekly).
4. **Narrative help** when asked: founder story for a specific meeting Shawn or Trinh sets (prep, not freelancing pitches).

### Out of scope (non-negotiable)
- Owning Sales, pipeline, forecast, or SQL accept/reject
- Pricing, proposals, or contract path (he can join a call; Shawn/Trinh own commercial)
- Running parallel campaigns, website changes, or “GTM experiments” without Shawn+Trinh approval
- Directing research delivery or staffing
- Opaque Innovation builds that never surface objectives, owners, and status to Trinh
- Creating urgency for the company without a dated ask in the intake tracker

### Operating rules (how he gets to “be Justin” without disruption)

| Rule | Detail |
|------|--------|
| **One intake** | Every intro Justin makes is logged within 48 hours in the shared tracker (contact, context, suggested next step). Shawn decides advance / nurture / kill. |
| **Hand off by meeting two** | Justin may own warmth through first conversation. By second substantive meeting, Shawn (or designee) is lead. Justin may stay as founder presence if useful. |
| **Time box exploration** | Any “dream” bet gets a 2–4 week scout charter: question, people to talk to, exit criteria, written readout to Trinh+Shawn. No open-ended crusades. |
| **Visibility** | Weekly 15-min sync: Justin → Shawn (intros made, status). Monthly: Justin → Trinh (themes + asks). No invisible workstreams. |
| **Optimism tax** | Claims about deal certainty or timelines go through Shawn’s qualification scorecard before they hit staff. |
| **Innovation firewall** | Panel Pro / Dalien-type work reports on a visible roadmap with Trinh as audience. Justin can champion ideas; he does not run a shadow company. If Innovation stays under him, it still follows the same visibility rules or it pauses. |

### What good looks like (so we can praise the right behavior)
- 4–8 quality intros per month into ICP or partner targets (quality > volume)
- 100% of intros logged
- Zero surprise “we already promised X” moments for Shawn/delivery
- Staff experience less thrash; Trinh can see Justin’s contribution without chasing

### What we stop asking him to be
- Head of Sales
- Head of Marketing ops
- Unilateral urgency engine
- Sole website gatekeeper without roadmap agreed with Shawn/Trinh
