# URI go-to-market strategy (draft)

Working draft for Tam to steer Trinh, Shawn (Sales as of Nov 1), and leadership. Built for a services firm that has grown on reputation and relationships, not a repeatable GTM system. Ad hoc intake is no longer enough: pipeline is thinning and growth has stalled.

**Horizon:** Build the machine in Q4 2026. Hit first operating targets in **Q1 2027** (Jan–Mar).

**Frameworks used (intentionally boring and proven):** ICP + beachhead market (Moore), Jobs-to-be-Done for offer focus, simple funnel stages, BANT/CHAMP-style qualification adapted for research services, MEDDPICC lite for larger deals, weekly pipeline cadence (force management).

## 1. What “GTM” means here (so the room doesn’t invent it)

Go-to-market is the system that answers five questions every week:

1. **Who** do we pursue (and who do we decline)?
2. **What** problem/offer do we lead with?
3. **How** do we create consistent demand (not hope)?
4. **How** do we qualify and advance deals?
5. **Who** owns each stage, and what is the cadence?

URI’s current default (inbound from relationships + opportunistic yes) is a **sales motion**, not a GTM system. Shawn owning Sales is necessary but not sufficient. Marketing tactics without ICP and qualification will recreate ad hoc with busier calendars.

## 2. Identify new market opportunities (before more tactics)

Use a fixed quarterly opportunity process. Do not brainstorm channels first.

### 2.1 Beachhead + ICP (Crossing the Chasm / practical ICP)

Pick **one primary beachhead** for Q1 (you can keep legacy work, but *growth* focus is one wedge).

Draft filters for URI (edit with Trinh/Shawn using evidence from best clients, e.g. Google-quality relationships vs Amazon-price dynamics):

| Dimension | Choose explicitly |
|-----------|-------------------|
| Industry / segment | e.g. AI product companies, enterprise platform PMs, PE-backed ops, regulated industries needing human evidence |
| Buyer | Economic buyer + research champion (title patterns) |
| Trigger | New AI feature, failed AI pilot, compliance/eval need, redesign, market entry |
| Deal profile | Min $X, timeline ≤Y months, URI differentiator required (quality / panel / human evidence) |
| Disqualifiers | Race-to-bottom price, “just recruit users,” no access to users/decision makers |

**Rule:** If a lead is outside the beachhead, it can still be accepted as overflow, but it does not get outbound or campaign investment in Q1.

### 2.2 Jobs-to-be-Done for offers

Map 3–5 jobs customers hire URI for (examples to pressure-test):

- “Help us know if this AI experience is good enough for real users before we scale.”
- “Give us credible human evidence our execs will trust.”
- “Find and run research with the right participants fast without killing quality.”
- “Tell us what to build next from user behavior, not opinions.”

Each job becomes a **named offer** (packaged scope, price band, typical timeline, proof). Ad hoc custom everything destroys forecasting and marketing.

### 2.3 Opportunity scan (quarterly, ½ day)

Once per quarter, leadership + Shawn + research lead:

1. List wins/losses last 12 months (why won/lost).
2. List adjacent segments where URI already has proof.
3. List 2–3 **partner channels** (FDE firms, design agencies, AI consultancies) who sell complementary last-mile work.
4. Score each opportunity: fit to ICP, URI right to win, urgency in market, path to 3+ deals in 6 months.
5. Pick **one growth bet** + **one partner bet** for the quarter. Kill the rest for now.

### 2.4 Where AI fits without confusing GTM

Separate:

- **Internal:** AI to deliver research faster/cheaper (margin, capacity).
- **External offer:** Human evidence for AI products/workflows (growth narrative).

Do not market “we do AI” as the wedge. Market **outcomes for users and decisions for builders**, with AI-era research as the context.

## 3. Generate leads (repeatable system, not random acts)

Build a **mixed demand engine**. Services firms that wait for inbound die slowly.

### 3.1 Channel mix for Q1 (suggested weight)

| Channel | Q1 role | Owner |
|---------|---------|-------|
| **1. Existing logo expansion / reactivation** | Fastest path to revenue and pipeline | Shawn + research directors |
| **2. Referral / partner** | Highest trust; includes FDE and agency partners | Tam/Trinh strategy; Shawn ops |
| **3. Outbound to ICP accounts** | Controlled pipeline creation | Shawn (+ light support) |
| **4. Thought leadership / content** | Mid-term reputation; supports outbound | Tam + designated researcher |
| **5. Events / community** | Selective; only ICP-dense rooms | Shawn |

Website-only “marketing” (Justin/David bottleneck) is not a channel strategy. Fix website as hygiene; do not wait on it to create pipeline.

### 3.2 Plays (concrete)

**Play A — Land and expand (weeks 1–12 of Q1)**  
- List top 20 accounts by historical $ and relationship health.  
- For each: one expansion hypothesis (new product surface, AI eval, continuous discovery retainer).  
- Cadence: 5 account reviews per week until all 20 have a next step or a kill reason.

**Play B — Reactivation**  
- Closed-lost and dormant 12–24 months.  
- Offer: short diagnostic / “AI experience readiness” conversation, not a capabilities deck.

**Play C — Partner-sourced**  
- 5 target partner types: FDE boutiques, product design agencies, AI implementation firms, PE operating partners, academic/industry research networks.  
- Offer them: URI as human-evidence and discovery partner (user on the other end).  
- Goal: 3 active partner conversations → 1 joint pursuit in Q1.

**Play D — ICP outbound**  
- Build a list of 50 ICP accounts (firmographics + trigger).  
- Sequence: value note → proof point → ask for problem conversation.  
- Volume target: e.g. 10 new conversations/month (tune to capacity).

**Play E — Proof content (light)**  
- One sharp proof asset per month (case vignette, teardown of AI UX failure modes, “how we evaluate human–AI readiness”).  
- Used in outbound and partner talks. Not a blog farm.

### 3.3 Lead definition (stop calling everything a lead)

- **Inquiry:** Anyone who asked.  
- **MQL:** Fits ICP filters + expressed a job we sell.  
- **SQL:** Shawn (or designee) accepts; discovery call booked or completed; problem + timing + buyer path plausible.  
- **Opportunity:** Scoped problem, estimated $, stage in CRM/sheet, next step dated.

No SQL, no celebration.

## 4. Qualify leads (so pipeline is real)

Use a **services-friendly scorecard**. Full MEDDPICC for large/complex; lighter CHAMP/BANT for smaller studies.

### 4.1 Minimum qualification (every SQL)

Must answer yes or “path to yes within 2 weeks”:

1. **Challenge:** Clear user/business problem URI can evidence (not “we need bodies in a study”).  
2. **Authority:** We can reach the person who can fund or strongly influence.  
3. **Money:** Budget exists or a credible path this quarter / next.  
4. **Priority:** Timeline and consequence of inaction.  
5. **Fit:** Matches beachhead or is an explicit exception Trinh/Shawn approve.

### 4.2 Disqualify hard (protect quality and margin)

- Price-only buyers (Amazon-pattern caution).  
- No access to users or decision makers.  
- “Validate our idea” with predetermined answer.  
- Scope that burns panel/recruiting brand for vanity metrics.

### 4.3 Stage model (keep it short)

1. **Discover** — Problem, users, success criteria.  
2. **Shape** — Approach, sample, timeline, commercial shape.  
3. **Propose** — Formal scope + $.  
4. **Commit** — Verbal/legal win path.  
5. **Deliver / Expand** — Delivery excellence + next opportunity.

Exit criteria per stage written in one page. No stage skipping without Shawn note.

| **Monthly ICP review** | Kill/add segments; refresh outbound list |
| **Quarterly opportunity scan** | Beachhead and partner bets |

Tooling can start as the shared Google Sheet (Objectives / Actions / pipeline tab). CRM later if volume demands it. Process first.
