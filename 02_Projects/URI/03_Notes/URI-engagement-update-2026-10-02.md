## Diagnostic update

The incomplete founder-to-CEO operating transition remains the leading diagnosis, but this week adds important contrary and refining evidence. Trinh has made visible decisions: she sponsored the readiness kickoff, permitted a bounded parallel period, and named Tam as the intended future execution lead. The unresolved test is no longer simply whether she will end Justin’s work immediately. It is whether she will define the evidence window, make and communicate the consolidation decision when it closes, and sustain the resulting authority structure.

The vision workshop is an intervention, not proof that the vision problem is solved. The readiness kickoff is activity, not proof of demand or delivery capability. Shawn’s reports are signals about leadership trust, not proof of resentment or undermining.

- Whether he listens, stays within the meeting objective, and adapts after facilitation or feedback.

### Diagnostic implication

These updates introduce two capability and operating-model questions without yet establishing either as a persistent problem. Trinh’s communication created real preparation friction, but her strategic position on the two AI efforts remains consistent. Lingan generated meeting friction and may be working from an adjacent rather than exact evidence base, but his fit should be tested through a clear role, feedback, and observable contribution rather than inferred from one kickoff.
