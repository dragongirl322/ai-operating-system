# Executive Advisor, Strategy & Growth

Advisor to the CEO · User Research International (URI)  
Role description · Tammy Snow

## Role purpose

Increase URI’s strategic clarity, growth quality, and execution capacity. Advise the CEO on where URI will play and how it will win; lead selected cross-functional strategic initiatives from diagnosis through decision and launch; and help transfer sustained execution to accountable URI leaders.

The role is designed to create enterprise value through clearer strategy, stronger retention and expansion, more disciplined go-to-market, stronger leadership follow-through, and a credible AI-era commercial agenda that a prospective buyer can understand and assess. 

AI is part of URI’s core growth strategy, not a separate initiative, lab, or business unit. (Note to self—not sure how this fits here)

## Partnership and engagement model

The primary partnership is with Trinh as CEO. Tam provides trusted counsel, surfaces risks and opportunities, frames consequential choices, and brings recommendations rather than waiting only for assignments.

The role combines senior advice with bounded leadership of initiatives explicitly designated by Trinh. Tam may lead an initiative through definition, alignment, planning, and launch without replacing the line leaders who own ongoing sales, delivery, people management, or functional results.

New work is accepted through explicit reprioritization with Trinh based on strategic value, urgency, and available capacity.

## Primary responsibilities

### 1. CEO counsel and strategic judgment

- Advise Trinh on company direction, priorities, organizational dynamics, and difficult decisions.
- Surface material risks, contradictions, and unresolved choices early.
- Help turn intentions into explicit commitments, decision points, milestones, and evidence.
- Support Trinh in strengthening leadership clarity and follow-through without substituting for the CEO’s authority or communication.

### 2. Strategy and transformation

- Partner with Trinh and the leadership team to clarify URI’s vision, strategic choices, ideal customers, offer priorities, and stop-doing decisions.
- Translate approved strategy into priorities, owners, milestones, measures, and an operating rhythm.
- Lead selected cross-functional strategic initiatives designated by Trinh through an agreed launch or transition milestone.
- Review progress and surface decisions or dependencies that require CEO action.

### 3. Growth-system coherence, including AI

- Shape an integrated growth agenda across existing-client retention and expansion, new business, go-to-market, and AI-era positioning and offers.
- Ensure that target buyers, offers, messaging, pipeline creation, sales motion, and delivery reinforce the same strategy.
- Support priority opportunities where senior strategic involvement can materially improve learning, trust, or commercial outcomes.
- Preserve clear ownership: sales leadership owns pipeline and sales execution; delivery leadership owns staffing, quality, and sustained delivery.

### 4. Selected client advisory and capability building

- Engage selectively with priority clients and prospects when doing so advances strategy, strengthens relationships, or tests a new offer.
- Help define and initially lead advisory consulting work that draws on Tam’s distinctive judgment and experience.
- Name an internal co-lead at the outset, make capability transfer explicit, and establish the milestone at which ongoing ownership transfers to URI.
- Extend Tam’s involvement beyond that milestone only through an explicit decision with Trinh.

### 5. Leadership guidance and facilitation

- Coach leaders on strategy-critical work, judgment, accountability, and execution.
- Facilitate workshops and leadership conversations when they directly support strategy, growth, or organizational follow-through.
- Leave formal performance management, compensation, staffing, and employment decisions with Trinh and the relevant line leaders.

## Decision rights and ownership

| Domain | Tam’s role | URI ownership |
|---|---|---|
| Company strategy and priorities | Diagnose, frame choices, recommend, facilitate, and track agreed commitments | Trinh makes material decisions; the leadership team executes |
| CEO-designated strategic initiatives | Lead definition, alignment, plan, milestones, and launch through an agreed transition point | Trinh sponsors and resolves material tradeoffs; named functional leaders own sustained execution |
| GTM and sales | Shape ICP, offers, positioning, sales motion, and operating model; support selected pursuits | Sales leadership owns pipeline, forecasting, selling activity, and sales results |
| Client advisory and delivery | Lead selected developmental or strategy-critical work and transfer capability | Delivery leadership owns staffing, quality, economics, and ongoing delivery |
| People and leadership | Advise, coach, and surface organizational risks | Trinh and line managers own performance, role, compensation, and employment decisions |
| Investment and organizational structure | Provide analysis and recommendations | Trinh decides, with owner or board involvement where required |

For initiatives Trinh explicitly assigns Tam to lead, Tam may:

- Convene the relevant leaders and working team.
- Request the information reasonably necessary to do the work.
- Develop the proposed plan, milestones, evidence requirements, and review cadence.
- Secure named owner commitments through the relevant line leaders.
- Escalate conflicting priorities, stalled decisions, and unresolved dependencies to Trinh.
- Represent an approved direction internally or with clients when agreed with Trinh.

## Initial designated priorities

- Facilitate the vision work and help convert the resulting direction into strategic choices, priorities, and stop-doing decisions.
- Lead definition and launch of URI’s proposed AI pivot with Trinh as sponsor and Shawn, Lingan, and other designated contributors.
- Help Trinh secure and communicate a dated disposition for the existing HITL Eval effort so URI does not operate competing shadow initiatives.
- Strengthen the integrated growth agenda across client retention and expansion, new business, GTM, and AI-era offers.
- Establish the baselines, owners, and operating cadence needed to assess progress.

## Operating requirements

The role depends on:

- Regular direct access to Trinh and a consistent CEO-advisor working cadence.
- Timely access to relevant financial, pipeline, client-health, initiative, and operating information.
- Participation from designated functional leaders and internal owners.
- One communicated mandate for each strategic initiative, including its sponsor, scope, decision rights, and transition point.
- Prompt CEO resolution of conflicting priorities, overlapping initiatives, or contested authority.
- Periodic review of priorities, contribution, capacity, and role boundaries.

## Out of scope unless explicitly added and reprioritized

- Standing up a separate AI business unit, lab, or HITL labor marketplace as a default mandate.
- Owning the full sales P&L, forecasting process, or ongoing sales organization as de facto CRO.
- Becoming the standing delivery owner for client work or the permanent operating owner of a new offer.
- Permanent people management of URI staff or taking over performance management from line leaders.
- Absorbing additional CEO-directed priorities without an explicit discussion of what will be deferred or stopped.

## Measures of success

Exact targets, baselines, attribution, and timing will be set with Trinh and the appropriate finance, sales, and delivery leaders.

### Initial 90-day evidence

- A concrete company direction with documented strategic choices, priorities, and stop-doing decisions.
- A dated decision and communication regarding the existing HITL Eval effort.
- An AI-pivot charter with target buyer and problem hypotheses, scope, owners, milestones, pilot or discovery plan, and evidence requirements.
- Baselines for revenue, qualified pipeline, client concentration, and priority-account health.
- Clearer ICP, offer hierarchy, and sales narrative ready for consistent use by leadership and sales.
- A leadership operating cadence that records owners, decisions, milestones, and follow-through.
- Confirmed access, initiative mandates, decision rights, and internal owners for Tam’s work.

### Subsequent value creation

- Improved retention, expansion, qualified pipeline, bookings, and delivery economics against agreed baselines.
- Reduced client-concentration exposure.
- Paid evidence of demand for URI’s AI-era offer, with explicit expand, revise, partner, or stop decisions.
- Faster and clearer strategic decisions with less cross-functional thrash.
- Greater leadership accountability and follow-through on strategy-critical work.
- Client advisory work that produces value and transfers capability to URI employees.
- Mission-aligned innovation that strengthens the core rather than diluting focus.
- A coherent external story: URI is a growth company with disciplined AI-era capabilities, not a firm running disconnected side projects.
- Greater transferability and enterprise value through clearer strategy, GTM, operating rhythm, and internal capability.

## Value to URI and its investors

- Adds senior strategy and growth capacity without confusing company governance.
- Reduces key-person overload on the CEO while strengthening, rather than replacing, her authority.
- Connects AI investment to buyer needs, commercial evidence, and business outcomes.
- Strengthens retention and expansion as well as focused new-business growth.
- Builds reusable internal advisory and delivery capability rather than long-term advisor dependency.
- Makes URI’s direction, decisions, operating discipline, and growth thesis easier to understand, diligence, and scale.
