With 0->1 products, you have to rely on opinion based decisions and those have to be led by one or two people who are charged w/ making those decisions based on good judgement and taste; you don't have data available to make those decisions.

This is particularly true in B2C because in order for a consumer to form a reliable opinion, they have to be exposed to the entire "system" of a solution and that can only happen once you ship it and give them an opportunity to form the opinion based on using the thing. 

Requires a lot of informed judgement from experts with experience and from prototyping and evaluating the problem thoroughly--it isn't just throwing spaghetti at the wall.

Decide what to build based on pain. Sometimes pain is unmet need (no tool to do the job); sometimes it is tech that didn't do enough; sometimes tech created the problem and people have habituated to it.

You build the product and ship it then fix the product and ship it then fix the business. iPod as an example. Targeting mac users only to increase mac sales but only capturing ~1% of market as result. By v3, built windows connector so bring people into Apple eco-system.
