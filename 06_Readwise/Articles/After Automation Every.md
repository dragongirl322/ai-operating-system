    - Note: Agents are AI workers that perform assigned tasks without a human in the immediate loop, producing outputs such as answers, actions, reports, drafts, or triage decisions. They appear in two main forms: coworker agents, which can be tagged and directed in ongoing work, and embedded agents, which operate inside product workflows as always-on helpers for repetitive tasks. They still require human oversight, maintenance, and final judgment to work well.
