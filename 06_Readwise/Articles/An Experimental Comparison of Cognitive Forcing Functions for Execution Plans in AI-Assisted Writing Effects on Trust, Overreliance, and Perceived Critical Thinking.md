# An Experimental Comparison of Cognitive Forcing Functions for Execution Plans in AI-Assisted Writing: Effects on Trust, Overreliance, and Perceived Critical Thinking

## Metadata
- Author: [[arxiv.org]]
- Full Title: An Experimental Comparison of Cognitive Forcing Functions for Execution Plans in AI-Assisted Writing: Effects on Trust, Overreliance, and Perceived Critical Thinking
- Category: #articles
- Summary: This study tested different cognitive forcing functions (CFFs) to help people critically evaluate AI-generated writing plans. The Assumptions CFF was most effective in reducing overreliance without adding mental effort. Overall, focusing on AI plans helped users better understand and trust AI outputs by encouraging reflection on the AI’s reasoning.
- URL: https://arxiv.org/html/2601.18033v1
