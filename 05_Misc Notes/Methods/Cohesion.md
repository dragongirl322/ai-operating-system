- _Look and feel is a system that is unified visually—colors, typeface, formats._
    
- _Using clear language consistently to communicate about products and services._
    

  

the act or state of sticking together tightly—_Merriam Webster_ 

  

Brand Cohesion=The act of displaying branding or brand details in a way that creates unity between them, _such as between brand perception and brand appearance_; establishing and/or maintaining a consistent message.--_[https://www.intimatology.com/got-brand-cohesion/](https://www.intimatology.com/got-brand-cohesion/)_

  

A key question for us to answer is this: what is the job of cohesiveness?

- For the customer, the job is to reduce barriers of using our products:
    

- We reduce need to satisfice (mix between satisfaction and suffice)—helping them choose travel products that meet their needs
    
- We reduce necessary work in understanding how to maximize value of $ spent
    
- We reduce effort in understanding emails, using our product, getting support/help when needed, dealing with changes, etc.
    

- For Expedia, the job is to improve salience which is achieved through mental and physical availability
