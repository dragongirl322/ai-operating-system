## Metadata
- Author: [[Lareina Yee, Anu Madgavkar, Sven Smit, Alexis Krivkovich, Michael Chui]]
- Full Title: Agents, Robots, and Us: Skill Partnerships in the Age of AI
- Category: #articles
- Summary: AI is changing how people work by partnering with intelligent machines like agents and robots. This shift means many jobs and skills will evolve, with humans focusing on guiding AI and using new skills like AI fluency and adaptability. To succeed, businesses must invest in both technology and worker training to create value together.

## Highlights
- **Demand for AI fluency—the ability to use and manage AI tools—has grown sevenfold in two years**, faster than for any other skill in US job postings. The surge is visible across industries and likely marks the beginning of much bigger changes ahead. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01kv24ks8bf3rwg5qhphanwvtr))
- A small set of skills is likely to remain uniquely human. These are rooted in social and emotional intelligence such as interpersonal conflict resolution and design thinking, which depend on empathy, creativity, and contextual understanding and will be challenging for machines to replicate.
