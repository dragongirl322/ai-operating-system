# Design Leader Brand

**Brand Essence (Your “North Star”)**

I cultivate clear eyes, an open mind, and a tender heart so I can cut through the noise and create meaning, contentment, and joy for myself—and help others do the same. With a blend of confident calm and authentic edginess that invites dialogue, I push boundaries to elevate people and organizations from strong to truly superb.

**Brand Pillars**

1. **_Elevating Excellence_**

2. What it Means: I challenge the status quo and relentlessly strive to take something strong and make it superb—whether that’s human centered design processes, products, or people. 
3. Why It Matters: Elevation fosters continuous improvement and innovation, driving outstanding results for my teams and the company.

4. **_Empowering Through Kindness & Courage_**

5. What it Means: I place genuine care for people at the forefront and am not afraid to be candid or taking daring steps for the greater good. I believe that treating others with kindness and respect unlocks trust and high performance. 
6. Why It Matters: Compassion creates trust while courage fuels transformative action. When combined, they shape a culture of loyalty and high performance.

7. **_Strategic Clarity & Badass Accountability_**

8. What it Means: As a decisive and strategic thinker, I set clear goals, hold myself and others accountable, and eliminate unnecessary complexity. 
9. Why It Matters: Clear, transparent objectives and processes ensure alignment, efficiency, and strong outcomes.

10. **_Authenticity & Individuality_**

11. What it Means: I am confident, calm, and credible with a daring edge. I march to the beat of my own drum, living my values with fearless authenticity that is clear in my leadership style.
12. Why It Matters: Authenticity and edgy self-expression builds trust and respect, inspiring others to be bold and innovative.

**Core Values**

1. Integrity: Upholding my values and demonstrating accountability in all actions. 
2. Curiosity: Remaining a lifelong learner with an open mind. 
3. Compassion: Practicing kindness and understanding toward others’ experiences.
4. Excellence: Continuously raising standards and inspiring others to do the same.
5. Family First:  Fostering relationships that prioritize personal and familial well-being.

**Brand Promise**

I promise to combine calm and fearless clarity with bold, barrier-breaking action. By uniting strategic insight, genuine care, and fearless innovation, I deliver high-impact results while empowering individuals to exceed even their own expectations.Through direct, unfiltered conversations and decisive action, I help create meaningful transformations for individuals, teams, and the organization as a whole.

**Key Messaging Guidelines**

1. Tone & Manner

2. Calm & Confident: Use a steady, measured tone, reflecting the influence of Stoicism. 
3. Fearless& Edgy: Show I'm willing to challenge conventions and take bold steps to achieve breakthrough results.
4. Warm & Approachable: Balance strong presence with open-hearted kindness and respect.
5. Strategic & Analytical: Ground statements in facts, data, and well-reasoned logic. 

6. Elevator Pitch: "I’m a design leader driven to fearlessly transform potential into outstanding performance. I combine clarity, compassion, and a healthy dose of badassery to drive innovation, inspire teams, and deliver undeniable results."
7. Talking Points

8. Leadership Philosophy: “My role is to elevate others and the experience practice by focusing on strengths, accountability, and continuous improvement.” 
9. Approach to Challenges: “I cut through complexities with a strategic, no-nonsense, data-informed approach and the guts to take decisive action--I get shit done.” 
10. Culture of Kindness& Boldness: “Kindness fosters trust, and courage ignites change. Both fuel the environment I strive to create.” 
11. Family & Personal Priorities: “I prioritize my family first; it’s what grounds me and aligns my values in both life and work.” 

**Brand Legacy Objectives**

1. Professional Legacy

2. Leadership: Be recognized as the best design leader in the company’s history by elevating Experience practices, nurturing talent, and delivering tangible business value. 
3. Mentorship & Culture: Cultivate a supportive environment where every team member can thrive and is motivated to excel.

4. Personal Legacy

5. Family Commitment: Demonstrate, through consistent actions, that my family is my foremost priority, preparing Tre to be a kind and effective business owner. 
6. Kindness & Character: Be remembered as someone who treats others with unwavering kindness, generosity, and respect.
